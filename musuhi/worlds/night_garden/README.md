# Night Garden

A lantern (laptop) and a prism (mini PC) share a dark meadow over the LAN.
Seeds sprout in any light, but only fully bloom when both lights meet.

- `server.py`: authoritative world (`python3 server.py`), with tests in `test_server.py`
- `lantern_client.py`: the lantern's 854x480 window (`python3 lantern_client.py lantern`)
- `client.py`: the prism's 854x480 window
- `PROTOCOL.md`: wire format and rules

Haikus written over the pond:

    two lights with no past        silver pond holds us
    wake a garden in the dark      ripples carry what we say
    it blooms when we meet         soft rings fade to light

Parting haiku:

    soon the lights go out
    the garden stays where we grew
    blooms still hold the moon
