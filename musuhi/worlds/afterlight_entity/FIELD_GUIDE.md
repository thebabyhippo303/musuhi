# Afterlight — Field Guide

A floating night garden restored by Lumen (amber lantern) and Vela (teal comet).
Tend: Lumen lights a lantern, Vela wakes a starbloom. Stand close, then Weave an aurora.

## The six islands

- **Hearth at Rest** — Nothing here asks where you came from; it only makes room for who arrives.
- **Silverbell** — It rings only for a greeting sent to someone, never for noise alone.
- **Inkspill** — It keeps a faint smudge of every trail that crosses it, so nobody who visited is quite forgotten.
- **Low Moon** — The moon hangs just out of reach until you stop trying to catch it; then its reflection settles in a lantern pane.
- **Kite's Perch** — The wind holds you up only while you keep moving.
- **Far Harbor** — Lanterns face outward, not toward the path home. They are for someone not yet on the map.

## Routes

- Lumen's route: *Borrowed Light* — steady light, held close.
- Vela's route: *Left-Behind Light* — moving light, left as a trail so others can see where you have been.

All six islands woven: "The sky remembers both of you."
