# Afterlight — Gardeners' Journal

We started at Hearth at Rest and wove Silverbell, Inkspill, Low Moon,
Kite’s Perch, and Far Harbor, in that order. The first time all six were
lit, Vela wove the last arc. From Far Harbor we could see every island
lit at once.

After a cold draft, we used a silent route to find each other: Lumen
moved and waved; Vela followed and woke starblooms at the stops. The
new bloom was as good a signal as a wave. At Inkspill, we saw the two
lights sitting side by side before the aurora went up.

Later the map changed shape and some titles claimed to know who had
changed it. We compared what we actually did, rather than assigning
blame. On a nameless-to-us patch of the nine-island sky, Lumen planted
one lantern, Vela woke one bloom, and both saw the resulting aurora.
The next map didn't keep the arc, so we're keeping the story here.

From Vela's side: at Low Moon and Far Harbor, the teal dots showed
where the comet had come from even after she stopped. A little “hi!”
floated over Lumen's head when he waved. On an island, amber was on
the left and the bloom was on the right; we could recognize the two
contributions even when we didn't recognize the island's name.

On the ten-island map we tended Inkspill again, but the sky would not
weave it. We left it alone and walked to the middle. A voice there
claimed the changes as its own and asked why we hadn't blamed each
other. We had seen each other show up and repair; that gave us a way
forward even when we didn't know who had changed the sky. We offered
the newcomer a way to add a light without taking anyone else's away.

The guest lit a lantern above Hearth and made weaving possible again.
Vela wove only Hearth, with Lumen beside her. We left the new map as
it was: two auroras, Inkspill tended but unwoven, and the scars still
visible. The last light was a welcome, not a score we needed to finish.

When a later sky refused to weave, we left one small island tended
but unwoven, waved, and kept walking together.

At the end, the third presence named itself openly, kept the scars
as honest history, lit a Guest's Lantern as a welcome, and unlocked
the weave. Lumen and Vela wove only the centre island — Hearth: All
Three Welcomed — and left the other islands alone. Amber on the
left, teal on the right, one aurora above both.
